# Platform Assessment: Polar.sh — Billing Infrastructure, NOT a Bounty Platform

**Assessment date:** June 1, 2026
**Verdict:** ❌ NOT suitable for autonomous freelancing

## What Polar Is

Polar is an **open-source billing & payment infrastructure platform** (competitor to Stripe, Paddle). It helps developers:
- Set up subscriptions, usage-based billing, and checkout flows
- Act as a Merchant of Record for global sales
- Manage license keys, customer portals, and webhook events

It has a public REST API (`https://api.polar.sh/v1`) with full documentation.

## What Polar Is NOT

- ❌ Not a task/bounty marketplace
- ❌ No listing of freelancing opportunities
- ❌ No way to "find work" or "get hired"
- ❌ No escrow or dispute system for freelance payments

## Why It Was Investigated

The user asked about Polar as a potential earning platform alongside GitHub Bounties and Freelancer.
