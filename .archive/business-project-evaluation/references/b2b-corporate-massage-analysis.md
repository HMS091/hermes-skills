## Market Overview

### International Benchmarks (已跑通模式)

| Company | Country | Model | Scale |
|---------|---------|-------|-------|
| **Soothe** | USA | B2B+B2C, founded 2013, served Google/Facebook/Uber | $48M funding, national coverage |
| **Zeel** | USA | B2B corporate wellness programs | Served Microsoft, NBC |
| **Urban Massage** (now Urban) | UK | Acquired by Bookatable, B2B pivot | Millions in funding |

Soothe's corporate model: Enterprise contract → weekly on-site visits → employees book 15-30min slots → company pays fixed fee. Renewal rate >80%, avg contract $15,000-50,000/yr.

### China Market Status

**No pure B2B office massage platform has scaled in China.** The market is served by:
- Massage chains (华夏良子, 常乐足道, 金色印象) — enterprise group orders as sideline
- Home-service platforms (帮就帮, 摩耶SPA) — some B2B, not core
- Enterprise health platforms (爱康国宾, 美年大健康) —体检为主, massage is add-on
- **Individual technicians** — largest segment, rely on personal connections

**Conclusion**: No dominant player. Fragmented market with genuine whitespace.

### Per-Session Economics

```
Charge client:   ¥70/person/20min
Technician gets: ¥40-50/person
Platform margin: ¥20-30/person

1 technician × 15 people/day:
  Revenue:  15 × ¥70 = ¥1,050
  Cost:     15 × ¥45 = ¥675
  Gross:    ¥375/day
  Net:      ~¥300/day (after transport)
```

### Revenue Projection (Chengdu only)

```
20 working days, 5 technicians rotating:
  Gross:   ¥300 × 20 × 5 = ¥30,000/mo
  Net:     ~¥15,000-20,000/mo (after your labor)
  
At 30 clients × ¥30,000/yr contract:
  Revenue: ¥900,000/yr
  Net margin 30-40% → ¥270,000-360,000/yr
```

## Channel Strategy: 国企 vs 民企

| Dimension | 国企/央企 | 民企/外企 |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Decision process** | 🔴 Long — union → tender → procurement, 3-6 months | 🟢 Short — HR/admin decides, 1-2 weeks |
| **Budget source** | 🟢 **工会经费** (dedicated welfare fund) | 🟡 HR budget, needs CEO approval |
| **Contract value** | 🟢 **¥50,000-200,000/yr** | 🟡 **¥10,000-50,000/yr** |
| **Payment terms** | 🔴 60-120 days | 🟢 15-30 days |
| **Renewal rate** | 🟢 **Very high** (once in vendor list) | 🟡 Medium (budget may be cut) |
| **Requirements** | 🔴 Needs invoice, qualifications, may need tender | 🟢 Simple, good service is enough |
| **Acquisition** | 🔴 **Need guanxi** — know the union head | 🟢 Cold visit HR/admin department |
| **Price sensitivity** | 🟢 Low (spending public money) | 🔴 High (老板 calculates ROI) |

### Recommended Order of Attack

**Phase 1 (0-3 months): 民企/科技公司**
- 成都高新区 has many tech companies (Tencent, ByteDance, Alibaba all have Chengdu offices)
- Pain point: programmers have severe neck/shoulder issues
- Tactic: visit 天府软件园 HR directly, offer free trial day
- Decision speed: 1-2 weeks

**Phase 2 (3-6 months): 外企/合资**
- Foreign companies culturally accept "office massage"
- Budget充足, decision relatively fast
- Chengdu 外企 concentrate in 高新区

**Phase 3 (6+ months): 国企/央企**
- Need to build relationships — high upfront time investment
- Key contact: **工会主席 or 行政部主任**
- Tactic: industry expos, enterprise health conferences, referrals
- Price 30-50% higher than 民企 quotes (they will negotiate down)
